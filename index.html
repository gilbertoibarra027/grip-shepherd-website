FAQ

Common Questions

How often should I regrip?

A common guideline is about every 30 to 40 rounds, or sooner if your grips feel slick, hard, or cracked. South Florida heat and humidity can wear grips faster, so if you play year-round, check them often.

What area do you serve?

We serve Weston and surrounding Broward County. Not sure if you're in range? Contact us and we'll let you know.

Can I bring my own grips?

Yes. If you already have grips you love, have them ready and we'll install them during your visit. Bring-your-own grips add +$10 per order to labor.

How much does a regrip cost?

Regrip labor starts at $4.99 per club. Grip prices depend on the grip you choose.

Is there a travel fee?

Within 10 miles of Weston, travel is free with a 5-club minimum. From 10 to 20 miles it's a $15 flat fee, waived at 13+ clubs; 20 to 30 miles is $25, waived at 20+ clubs. Beyond 30 miles we'll send a custom quote or require a $150 minimum order.
